# ADPKD Research Alert — 2026-05-28

## ALERT — Pipeline-Relevant — MANGROVE Phase 2a: CFTR inhibitor GLPG2737 shows no efficacy in ADPKD

**What happened:** Gansevoort et al. (NDT, May 2026) published the full results of the MANGROVE Phase 2a randomized double-blind trial (NCT04578548, n=66), showing that GLPG2737 (a CFTR inhibitor, 150 mg/day) did not reduce htTKV growth vs placebo over 52 weeks in rapidly progressive ADPKD (GLPG2737 +8.2% vs placebo +9.2%; difference −0.91%, p=0.61), with no significant eGFR benefit (difference −2.31 mL/min/1.73m², p=0.17); the study was terminated early due to lack of efficacy.

**Why people may care:** CFTR inhibition had robust preclinical support and mechanistic rationale for reducing cyst fluid secretion; this negative RCT removes GLPG2737 at this dose from the ADPKD pipeline and materially weakens the broader CFTR-inhibition hypothesis for slowing cyst growth in humans.

**What limits confidence:** Phase 2a exploratory design with a small sample size (n=66, underpowered for definitive clinical conclusions); htTKV and eGFR are surrogate endpoints, not patient-important outcomes; only a single dose was tested (150 mg/day) — whether higher doses, different CFTR modulators, or combination regimens with tolvaptan might still show benefit cannot be excluded from this data alone.

**Classification:** pipeline-relevant (negative milestone — removes CFTR inhibition at this dose from near-term ADPKD development; prunes hypothesis space)

**Study design:** Phase 2a randomized double-blind placebo-controlled trial (2:1 allocation); 52-week double-blind period followed by 52-week open-label extension (OLE terminated early); 4-week follow-up per period
**Publication event:** full-paper-published (NDT, peer-reviewed, full methods and data)
**Endpoint:** surrogate — htTKV (primary); eGFR change (key secondary)
**Comparator:** placebo
**Result:** negative (both primary and secondary endpoints)
**Skepticism flags:** Small n (n=66); surrogate endpoints only; company-funded study of sponsor compound (Galapagos); single dose tested

**Context note:** The companion preclinical paper (Ganapathy et al., Am J Nephrol, April 2025; pm_40300563) showed GLPG2737 prevents cyst growth in multiple mouse models and human ADPKD kidney cells. The failure to translate to humans is consistent with ADPKD's historically poor preclinical-to-clinical record. The trial registry (NCT04578548) had been listed as TERMINATED since mid-2024 but the full peer-reviewed results are newly available with this publication.
