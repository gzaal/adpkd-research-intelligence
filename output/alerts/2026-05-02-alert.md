# ADPKD Scan Alert — 2026-05-02

## ALERT — Practice-relevant — JASN publishes externally-validated multivariable kidney failure prediction model for early ADPKD

**What happened:** Ravichandran et al. (JASN, May 2026) developed a multivariable Cox prediction model for kidney failure (eGFR <15, dialysis, or transplant) in CRISP+HALT-A (n=759, baseline eGFR 91 mL/min/1.73 m², median 10.2-year follow-up) using age, sex, eGFR, ADPKD genotype, and Mayo Imaging Class, and externally validated it in the Mayo clinical registry (ages 15–49, eGFR ≥60).

**Why people may care:** Early-stage ADPKD risk stratification is a long-standing unmet need driving tolvaptan eligibility decisions, trial enrichment, and patient counselling; this is the largest externally-validated effort to date that combines routinely-available clinical data with Mayo Imaging Class for the patient-important endpoint of kidney failure.

**What limits confidence:** Observational design with no intervention arm; external validation cohort restricted to ages 15–49 with preserved eGFR (eGFR ≥60), so generalizability to older patients, advanced disease, and non-Caucasian populations remains to be demonstrated; downstream calibration drift in non-research settings has not been tested.

**Classification:** practice-relevant

**Study design:** Prospective observational prognostic-model development (CRISP + HALT-A) with external validation in the Mayo ADPKD registry
**Publication event:** full-paper-published
**Endpoint:** patient-important — kidney failure (eGFR <15 mL/min/1.73 m², dialysis, or transplant)
**Comparator:** none (single-cohort prognostic model; compared only to itself across cohorts)
**Result:** positive — good discrimination and calibration over a 15-year horizon
**Skepticism flags:** Observational; external validation restricted to early-stage / younger subset; potential industry conflicts among co-authors typical for ADPKD trial-affiliated investigators
