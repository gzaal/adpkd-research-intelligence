## Highlights

### 1. Largest pediatric ADPKD macroscopic-hematuria series links MaHu to higher cyst burden and antihypertensive use

- **What happened:** Seeman et al. (European Journal of Pediatrics, May 7 2026, pm_42098312) report a four-centre European retrospective analysis of 289 children (median age 10.2 y) with ADPKD. Macroscopic hematuria (MaHu) occurred in 21/289 (7.2%) and was the second most frequent presenting symptom (11/51). Compared with MaHu− children, MaHu+ children had higher antihypertensive use (14.3% vs 3.1%, p=0.017), more often had BP ≥75th percentile (95% vs 64%, p=0.002), and had more cysts (median 20 vs 10, p=0.038).
- **Why people may care:** Pediatric ADPKD evidence is sparse. This is the largest pediatric MaHu series to date and gives nephrologists a numeric anchor when counselling families after a hematuria episode in a child with ADPKD. It supports a closer-monitoring posture for blood pressure and cyst progression in MaHu+ children, consistent with the KDIGO 2025 emphasis on early BP detection in pediatric ADPKD.
- **What limits confidence:** Retrospective; tertiary referral centres likely over-represent more advanced pediatric disease; only 21 MaHu+ cases mean subgroup p-values are sensitive to a few patients; the design cannot disentangle whether elevated BP precedes or follows MaHu; no longitudinal eGFR/TKV outcome data tied to MaHu status.
- **Classification:** hypothesis-generating
- **Scores:** Importance 5/10 | Evidence 5/10 | Novelty 7/10 | Decision 5/10 | Calibration 9/10

### 2. RETRACTED: 2020 miR-25-3p autophagy paper in PKD mice withdrawn — small but clean negative for a preclinical mechanism claim

- **What happened:** *Renal Failure* (May 6 2026, pm_42091106) issued a retraction of the 2020 paper "miR-25-3p promotes proliferation and inhibits autophagy of renal cells in polycystic kidney mice by regulating ATG14-Beclin 1" (PMID 32340512). The retraction notice itself carries no new mechanistic data.
- **Why people may care:** ADPKD's preclinical literature has a poor translation record, partly because of fragile single-paper mechanism claims. Retractions are the cleanest possible negative event for a preclinical hypothesis: they remove an item from the dataset rather than complicating it. The miR-25-3p / ATG14-Beclin axis should not be cited as supporting evidence in any future ADPKD synthesis. It also provides a useful general reminder when reading preclinical autophagy / miRNA papers in the field.
- **What limits confidence:** None for the retraction itself — this is a publication event, not a scientific claim. The decision relevance is limited because miR-25-3p was never a clinical-pipeline candidate; the broader caution about preclinical literature integrity is the larger takeaway.
- **Classification:** tracker maintenance only (with skepticism note)
- **Scores:** Importance 2/10 | Evidence — n/a | Novelty 6/10 | Decision 3/10 | Calibration 9/10

### 3. Inflammation and innate immunity emerge as a coherent ADPKD research theme — three convergent preclinical signals, no clinical implications yet

- **What happened:** Three concurrent reports plus one title-only paper outline an inflammation/innate-immunity axis in ADPKD biology. (a) Wei et al. (IJMS May 2026, ss_a0327d4970ec) show that the synthetic TLR2 agonist Pam3CSK4 amplifies NF-κB, mTOR, and c-Myc/Wnt signalling and accelerates cyst growth and renal fibrosis in Pkd1RC/RC mice; endogenous TLR2 ligands decorin and biglycan are elevated in human PKD kidneys. (b) Yashchenko et al. (Kidney360 May 2026, pm_42092347) describe a sex-specific deficit in kidney resident macrophage (KRM) niche refilling in Pkd1 cystic mice, with female cystic mice failing to rebuild the niche by 90 days post-clodronate depletion; delayed niche filling tracked with worse cystic index, fibrosis, and kidney function. (c) Simankowicz et al. (IJMS May 2026, ss_a4f2973499) report a case-control study (n=60 ADPKD vs 45 controls) showing lower plasma anandamide and 2-arachidonoylglycerol (p<0.001) and higher IL-6 (p<0.001) in ADPKD; companion translational paper (Betkar/Tam, Mol Med March 2026, ss_a6ae244ffc) reports progressive endocannabinoid system dysregulation with disease severity and proposes CB1R as a candidate target. (d) A title-only Journal of Nephrology paper (Juanlu et al. May 2026, pm_42096327) proposes an interferon → NETosis → IL-6 → epithelial-mesenchymal-transition axis as defining an early therapeutic window.
- **Why people may care:** Multiple groups are independently arriving at inflammation and innate immunity as modifiers of ADPKD progression. This is a research-prioritization signal rather than a clinical signal: NLRP3 inflammasome inhibitors, TLR2 antagonists, and CB1R-directed agents are tractable from a drug-development perspective.
- **What limits confidence:** Wei et al. is an agonist-driven design with no antagonist arm — it cannot establish that TLR2 blockade is therapeutic. Yashchenko et al. is mechanistic mouse work with no proposed druggable handle. Simankowicz et al. is cross-sectional, single-centre, with no longitudinal eGFR/TKV outcomes. Juanlu et al. is title-only with promotional framing flagged. ADPKD's preclinical-to-clinical translation history is poor.
- **Classification:** hypothesis-generating
- **Scores (cluster average):** Importance 4/10 | Evidence 3/10 | Novelty 7/10 | Decision 3/10 | Calibration 7/10

## Evolving Understanding

### Pediatric ADPKD — first quantitative anchor for hematuria as a "more-advanced-disease" marker
The Seeman MaHu paper does not change any KDIGO 2025 recommendation, but it gives clinicians a numeric anchor for counselling: in a child with ADPKD presenting with macroscopic hematuria, the prior probability of higher cyst burden and elevated BP is meaningfully higher than in a MaHu− child with the same age. The clinical implication is closer BP and imaging follow-up after a MaHu episode in childhood. This complements existing pediatric infrastructure (NCT04782258 pediatric tolvaptan, NCT07260071 King's College pediatric BP monitoring, NCT04338048 CHOP ADPKD).

### Inflammation and innate immunity — a research theme is forming, but it is not yet a clinical hypothesis
The TLR2 (Wei), KRM niche (Yashchenko), and endocannabinoid/IL-6 (Simankowicz/Betkar) papers are independent groups arriving at related ideas in the same digest window. Combined with prior NLRP3 inflammasome work, inflammation is becoming a coherent ADPKD research axis. **What this is:** a research-prioritization signal — these targets are tractable for early drug development, and the convergence is not coincidence. **What this is not:** a clinical signal. None of these reports identifies a tractable druggable handle that has been validated in ADPKD outcomes; the human cross-sectional ECS data is not longitudinal; the TLR2 work is agonist-driven; the KRM work is mechanistic without an intervention. ADPKD's preclinical-to-clinical translation history is poor enough that mechanistic convergence alone should not raise clinical confidence in any specific intervention.

### Polycystin restoration — methodology pieces continue to accumulate
The Chakraborty/Yu mini-CRISPRa paper is methodologic infrastructure for non-AAV CRISPRa polycystin restoration. It does not move the clinical pipeline directly, but it lowers the technical barrier for academic groups to test additional polycystin-restoration angles. This complements the Lakhia six-nucleotide-motif disruption (W18) and the Vertex VX-407 small-molecule corrector / Regulus farabursen miR-17 inhibitor / PYC-003 RNA programs. The pattern of independent mechanisms all converging on "more polycystin = less cyst" is consistent with the polycystin-restoration thesis.

### Diagnostic genetics — long-read sequencing for the residual unsolved cases
The Yale HiFi long-read abstract (ss_44af34eb54) extends the diagnostic-yield narrative built by the Genetics in Medicine optimization paper (W18, pm_41653026): once short-read ES is pseudogene-aware and depth-optimized, the next yield gains likely come from long-read sequencing of structural variants. Until methods/yield are published in full, this is hypothesis-generating only. The G-531 PKD-to-Alport reclassification case (ss_41b35b7130) is a single example, but it lines up with the broader KDIGO 2025 message and the Caliskan kidney-transplant-candidate cohort (W17 backfill, pm_42017070): genetic testing should be considered in atypical or ambiguous PKD presentations.

### Retraction of miR-25-3p / ATG14-Beclin — small but clean
The retraction is an opportunity to do a "what should I stop citing" pass. miR-25-3p / ATG14-Beclin should not be carried forward in any synthesis. The broader caution applies: small preclinical mouse miRNA papers should be load-bearing for hypotheses, not for clinical inferences.

## Skepticism Notes

1. **pm_42096327 (Juanlu interferon-NETosis-IL-6-EMT axis, J Nephrology May 2026).** Title-only entry; no abstract indexed; the framing "defines the early therapeutic window in ADPKD" is promotional in advance of any indexed data. Treat as a placeholder pending full text. Claim calibration: 5/10.

2. **ss_a0327d4970ec (Wei TLR2 agonist).** The design uses a synthetic TLR2 agonist (Pam3CSK4) without an antagonist arm. Showing that adding TLR2 stimulation accelerates cysts is not the same as showing that blocking TLR2 reduces cysts. The decorin/biglycan upregulation in human PKD kidneys is suggestive but cannot be used to recommend TLR2-blocking therapy.

3. **ss_a4f2973499 / ss_a6ae244ffc (Endocannabinoid system papers).** The IJMS clinical case-control is single-centre, cross-sectional, with no longitudinal outcome data; the Mol Med translational work is preclinical. The headline that "ECS modulates inflammation in ADPKD" is plausible but untested as an intervention.

4. **ss_55c183d4d3 (G-524 "Synergistic Pathogenicity in Adult PKD: An Index Case").** A single index case cannot establish synergistic pathogenicity. Avoid the headline; flag for skepticism.

5. **General preclinical-to-clinical translation caveat for the inflammation cluster.** TLR2, KRM niche, and ECS targets are mechanistically interesting but ADPKD's translation history is poor. Mechanistic convergence is a research-prioritization signal, not a clinical confidence builder.

6. **pm_42091106 retraction reminder.** When citing preclinical autophagy/miRNA papers in ADPKD, prefer recent reviews and replication studies over individual older papers — multiple field-internal retractions in recent years.
